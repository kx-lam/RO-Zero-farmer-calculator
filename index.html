Presets are the max-level skills from the rozerodb skill tree (Ragnarok Zero), with your 1st-job skills included. Change any number to match your skill level. Mastery ATK is the flat bonus from passives (Sword/Spear/Axe Mastery, Iron Fists, Demon Bane…); melee/ranged % and skill % are gear lines like "+10% melee physical damage". ATK % from gear is already inside the ATK your status window shows. CRIT only counts for basic attacks (×2 with a katar). Cast: rozerodb gives one cast time, so set how much of it is fixed (renewal is usually 20%); the rest shrinks with DEX/INT. After-cast delay −% comes from gear like Kiel-D-01. Each use takes cast time, then the longest of your attack motion (ASPD) and the delay, which run at the same time (Bahamut guide). Damage % boxes are separate multipliers, so spreading bonuses across them beats stacking one.

Formulas follow roz.prontera.info/mechanics. Physical: (weapon ATK × (1 + STR/200, or DEX/200 for bows, instruments and whips) × size × element + (other gear ATK + 2 × status ATK) × Neutral element) × skill % × your bonuses × (4000+DEF)/(4000+10·DEF) − (monster Lv + VIT)/2, times hit chance (100 + HIT − monster "100% hit"). Magic: (MATK × skill % × (1000+MDEF)/(1000+10·MDEF) − (monster Lv + INT)/4) × element, always hits. Your dodge: 95 + FLEE − monster "95% flee". Cast time × (1 − √((2·DEX + INT)/530)). Status ATK/MATK/HIT/FLEE/soft DEF from stats use the verified formulas on that page. Basic attacks and skills were checked against the Landgris ROCalculator (Zero mode) and match its average damage. Weapon damage variance is left out. Area skills split their time across the monsters they hit. EXP tables cover base Lv 1–70 and job levels from the official guide.
