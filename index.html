Attack details
Presets are max-level skills from the rozerodb skill tree; change any number to match yours. Mastery ATK is the flat bonus from weapon passives; melee/ranged % and skill % are gear lines. ATK % from gear is already in your status window. CRIT only counts for basic attacks. Fixed share of cast is the part DEX/INT can't shrink (usually 20%). Each damage % box is its own multiplier, so spreading bonuses beats stacking one.
How damage is worked out
From roz.prontera.info/mechanics, checked against the Landgris ROCalculator (Zero). Physical: ATK × size × element × skill % × bonuses, reduced by DEF, × hit chance. Magic: MATK × skill % reduced by MDEF × element, always hits. Cast × (1 − √((2·DEX + INT)/530)). Average damage only; area skills split time across targets.

Zeny Hunter

#Map / monsterEarns fromNet zeny / hrLoot / hrCosts / hrKills / hrZeny / killEXP / minHP lost / min
Open maps (or monsters farmed on their own) ranked by net zeny / hr with your current attack: loot value per kill (rozerodb, with your drop rate bonus) less skill costs like Mammonite, minus SP items and the consumables you have switched on. Monsters rozerodb has no EXP for yet still count, since they drop loot. Bosses and skipped monsters are left out. Monsters farmed on their own need that many spawns on their best open map, since rare spawns take far longer to find than your walking time assumes. Click a map to open it in the map planner, or a monster to pick it.
